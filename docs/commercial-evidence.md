# Conversion content: owner review and missing evidence

The owner requested a local implementation of the conversion audit, with final inclusion to be decided later. Benefits, qualification prompts, measurement frameworks, commercial questions and access questions are editorial drafts. They do not establish results, integrations, numerical service levels or internal controls.

## Implemented
- Home: problem-to-objective rows, measurement overview, client benefit and service scorecard links.
- CEX and DEX: best-fit situations, intended benefits, exact-setup qualification and five proposed metric definitions each.
- How We Work: capital/fee model, commercial allocation questions, permission and escalation questions, initial-discussion expectations.
- Company: confirmed operating model and a place for team/accountability evidence.
- Shared enquiry: an email briefing template with optional capital range and no credential request.
- Development-only notes mark evidence gaps in their intended page context. Static builds exclude these notes.

## Evidence required before stronger claims

| Gap | Required inputs | Intended placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team | Approved names, roles, biographies, dates, public profiles and relevant operating responsibilities | Company; short homepage introduction |
| Firm history | Contracting entity, registration/location, operating history; separation from founders' prior work | Company |
| Client results | Permission, market/venue/pool, period, capital, constraints, intervention, baseline/outcome, methodology and limitations | Homepage and service case study |
| References | Exact approved quotation, attribution and permission | Relevant case study |
| Coverage | Exchange/chain/DEX/pool version; current, historical or assessment required; exclusions | Service coverage |
| Economics | Fee model, minimum capital, duration, cost/P&L/rebate/LP fee allocation, withdrawal and exit terms | How We Work |
| Controls | Permitted actions, approval/revocation authority, pause/restart procedure, enforced limits, monitoring and escalation | How We Work |
| Reporting | Real sanitised screenshot/recording, history, refresh cadence, export scope and CEX/DEX differences | Reporting section |
| KPI availability | Measures collected today, formulas, sources, sampling, attribution, exclusions and cadence | Measurement sections |
| Latency | Execution path, venue, period, sampling, distribution and limitations | CEX evidence |

No answers are inferred from competitor copy, chart animation or use of Safe/Squads.
